The scraper that produced these files is scrape.py with jma.py. The analysis scripts are weekly_odds.py and enso_composite.py, and their output is analysis.json.

Things to know before you use it

Town, not mountain. Every station is in a valley town. Resorts above them get more snow and stay colder. Compare a station with itself across years, not with a resort's own figures.

A change at Kutchan in 2005. The agency marks the Kutchan snowfall series as not comparable across the winter of 2005-06, when the measuring method changed. Snow on the ground has no such mark. The analysis never compares a winter with neighbours on the other side of that change.

Empty cells. At staffed stations the agency writes a dash when nothing fell. The files keep those cells empty, and this page reads them as zero when the rest of the day's row is present. Rows the agency marks as incomplete carry a 1 in the flag column and are left out of the charts.
