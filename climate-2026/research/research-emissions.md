**Has the growth slowed? Is a peak in sight?**
- Growth has slowed. Fossil CO2 grew about 1.1% in 2025 and is projected to fall 0.4% in 2026. Total CO2 is still rising (+1.3%) because of land use, and total GHG was flat in 2025. GCP authors say a rebound is likely. The 2026 fall depends partly on the oil shock. Assessment based on the sources above.
- UNFCCC NDC synthesis (reported 27 Oct 2026): if new NDCs are met, total GHG peaks before 2035 and falls to about 6% below 2019 in 2035 (range 0 to 12%). The 2025 synthesis gave about 12% below 2019. VERIFIED (snippet, secondary): https://www.universalassetowners.com/newsroom/ndc-synthesis-6-percent-wmo-co2-record-cop31/

## 3. China and India

**China**
- 2025: CO2 fell about 0.3% (Q4 -1%). This was the first annual fall since 2022 and, per AFP, the first flat or falling calendar year while energy demand rose. Fossil-fuel CO2 +0.1%; cement -7%; transport -3%; power -1.5%; chemicals +12%. Power demand rose 520 TWh. Carbon intensity -4.7% in 2025 and -12% over 2020-25, against a target of -18%. Authors: "not yet clear that emissions have peaked". VERIFIED (snippet): https://www.carbonbrief.org/analysis-chinas-co2-emissions-have-now-been-flat-or-falling-for-21-months/ ; https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-12/china-s-emissions-fell-last-year-in-first-decline-since-2022
- 2026 by quarter (CREA/Myllyvirta for Carbon Brief): Q1 +2% (coal plants run inflexibly, wind and solar output wasted); Q2 -1% (the first time an oil-use fall drove a total fall, because of the Hormuz crisis); Q3 -3.1% (the largest quarterly fall since 2022). Year to date about -0.5%. Flat or falling since March 2024. Myllyvirta expects oil use to rebound when prices fall. VERIFIED (snippet): https://www.carbonbrief.org/analysis-chinas-quarterly-co2-falls-3-in-largest-drop-since-covid ; https://energyandcleanair.org/analysis-chinas-co2-climbs-2-in-early-2026-due-to-wasted-wind-and-solar/ ; https://www.carbonbrief.org/analysis-chinas-co2-emissions-fall-in-q2-2026-due-to-plummeting-oil-use

- 2035 NDC (announced by Xi in Sep 2025; official document 3 Nov 2025): cut economy-wide net GHG 7 to 10% from peak by 2035, with no peak year stated; non-fossil share of energy above 30%. This is the first absolute target. Analysts say a 1.5 C path needs about -30%. VERIFIED (snippet): https://www.carbonbrief.org/qa-what-does-chinas-new-paris-agreement-pledge-mean-for-climate-action/ ; https://www.climatechangenews.com/2025/09/24/china-unveils-underwhelming-emissions-cutting-target-for-2035/

## 6. Projected warming by 2100

- **UNEP EGR 2026 (21 Oct 2026):** current policies about 2.8 C; full NDCs about 2.6 C; plus net-zero pledges about 2.1 C. Under current policies, the chance of staying below 2 C is about 6% and the chance of exceeding 3 C is about 1 in 4. A 1.5 C path with the lowest overshoot needs -18% by 2030, -45% by 2035 and -69% by 2040 from 2025 levels, with a peak of about 1.7 to 1.8 C and a return to 1.5 C by 2100. 1.5 C with no or limited overshoot is no longer possible. Each 5 years of current emissions adds about 0.1 C to peak warming. VERIFIED (snippet): https://www.unep.org/en/news-and-stories/press-release/new-unep-report-lays-out-emission-cuts-needed-return-15degc ; https://sdg.iisd.org/news/emissions-gap-report-2026-aims-for-lowest-possible-15c-overshoot/ ; https://healthpolicy-watch.news/global-emissions-must-halve-within-decade/
  - CONFLICT: the 2.6 C NDC figure for 2026 is higher than the 2.3 to 2.5 C in EGR 2025. The scenario definitions (conditional or unconditional) may differ. Check the report.
- **UNEP EGR 2025 (4 Nov 2025):** NDCs 2.3 to 2.5 C (down from 2.6 to 2.8 C in 2024); current policies 2.8 C (down from 3.1 C). Part of the fall is methodology. The US withdrawal offsets about 0.1 C. VERIFIED (snippet): https://www.unep.org/en/publications/report/emissions-gap-report-2025

- **IEA WEO 2025 (Nov 2025):** STEPS about 2.5 C by 2100 (from 2.4 C); the revived Current Policies Scenario about 2.9 C. VERIFIED (snippet): https://www.carbonbrief.org/iea-fossil-fuel-use-will-peak-before-2030-unless-stated-policies-are-abandoned/

## 8. Carbon pricing and subsidies

- World Bank State and Trends of Carbon Pricing 2026 (19 May 2026): direct carbon pricing covers just over 29% of global GHG, or about one third if planned instruments start. There are 87 instruments (+7). Revenue was more than $107bn in 2025 (under $30bn in 2016; about +2% real). ETSs give more than 70% of revenue. The average price is nearly $21/tCO2e (+7% in a year; doubled in 10 years). Credit issuance +8%. ## 11. Deforestation and land sink

- GFW/WRI (29 Apr 2026, 2025 data): tropical primary forest loss 4.3 Mha (from 6.7 Mha record in 2024, -36%). This is still 46% above 10 years ago. Fire-driven tropical primary loss -50%. Fire was 42% of total global tree cover loss of 25.5 Mha. Brazil primary loss -42%, and its non-fire loss was the lowest on record. Bolivia was second with 620,630 ha. The 2030 zero-deforestation goal is off track.
