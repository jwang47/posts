## 6. Agriculture, food, land
- **Food systems:** about 34% of anthropogenic GHG in 2015 (18 GtCO2e), down from 44% in 1990 (Crippa et al. 2021, Nature Food / EDGAR-FOOD). The IPCC SRCCL range is 21–37%. Methane from livestock and rice is about 35% of food-system emissions. Livestock is about one third of anthropogenic methane (UNEP/CCAC 2021). — https://www.carbonbrief.org/food-systems-responsible-for-one-third-of-human-caused-emissions/ ; https://www.fao.org/newsroom/detail/Food-systems-account-for-more-than-one-third-of-global-greenhouse-gas-emissions — VERIFIED (snippet)
- **Feed additives:** 3-NOP (Bovaer) cuts methane yield by about 26% in dairy at 60 mg/kg DMI (Frontiers in Animal Science 2025 meta-analysis, 30 studies). Other meta-analyses: Penn State about 28%; UC Davis about 33%. The effect falls with higher fibre in the diet. The manufacturer claims about 30% (dairy) and 45% (feedlot). Hard to use in grazing systems (UNVERIFIED). — https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/animal-science/articles/10.3389/fanim.2025.1689264/full ; https://dairy.osu.edu/newsletter/buckeye-dairy-news/volume-26-issue-3/3-nop-bovaer%E2%84%A2-receives-fda-approval — VERIFIED (snippet)
- Asparagopsis (red seaweed, bromoform): large reductions in trials (often >50%), but the effect fades, and there are supply and bromoform-safety questions. — UNVERIFIED (memory)
- **Food waste:** 1.05 bn t in 2022, about 19% of food available to consumers (132 kg/person). Households 631 Mt (60%), food service 290 Mt, retail 131 Mt. ## 10. Solar radiation management
- **Basics (UNVERIFIED/standard science):** SAI puts SO2 (or calcite and similar) into the lower stratosphere (about 20 km). Pinatubo (1991) cooled the world by about 0.5 °C for about a year. ## 12. Economics
