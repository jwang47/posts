## 4. Is warming accelerating?

- **Hansen et al. 2025**, "Global Warming Has Accelerated: Are the United Nations and the Public Well-Informed?", *Environment: Science and Policy for Sustainable Development* 67(1), doi 10.1080/00139157.2025.2434494. Since 2010 the warming rate has risen by more than 50% over the 1970-2010 rate of 0.18 C per decade. The paper blames mainly cuts in ship aerosol (IMO 2020), at about +0.2 C. It argues that aerosol cooling is above 1 C, against the mainstream estimate of about 0.5 C, and that ECS is high. https://www.columbia.edu/~jeh1/mailings/2025/Hansen_etal2025.Envir_Global.Warming.Acceleration_FULL.abs.main.SM.pdf ; https://www.climatecodered.org/2025/04/james-hansen-and-scientific-contest.html. VERIFIED (snippet)
- **Hansen et al. 2023 ECS: 4.8 +/- 1.2 C**, mostly from the glacial-interglacial record. https://www.carbonbrief.org/guest-post-why-low-end-climate-sensitivity-can-now-be-ruled-out (from the search summary; the attribution is mixed). VERIFIED (snippet), weak
- **Mainstream view:** human-induced warming is 0.27 C per decade (IGCC 2025), which is faster, but it fits the expected effect of rising GHGs and falling aerosol. Forster (IGCC 2023): not "what you'd expect from a world accelerating or heading toward a tipping point". Hausfather and Overpeck disagreed. https://phys.org/news/2024-06-earth-evidence-climate.pdf. VERIFIED (snippet)
- **Statistical debate.** Beaulieu et al. 2024, "A recent surge in global warming is not detectable yet", found no significant acceleration in the raw series. Foster and Rahmstorf (GRL, 2026) remove ENSO, volcanic and solar signals, and find a significant acceleration since about 2015 (more than 98% confidence); Carbon Brief says the pace "nearly doubled since 2015". Critics: the statistician Robert Lund, and a non-peer-reviewed preprint by Triacca and Pasini (2026) that claims spurious changepoints. https://news.agu.org/press-release/in-a-first-researchers-confirm-global-warming-has-accelerated-in-last-decade/ ; https://www.realclimate.org/index.php/archives/2026/03/how-robust-is-our-accelerometer/ ; https://www.carbonbrief.org/pace-of-global-warming-has-nearly-doubled-since-2015-study-says/ ; https://www.livescience.com/planet-earth/climate-change/the-warming-trend-nearly-doubled-after-2014-the-rate-of-global-warming-has-accelerated-more-in-the-past-decade-than-ever-before. VERIFIED (snippet)
- **Earth's energy imbalance (Mauritsen et al. 2025, AGU Advances 6, e2024AV001636):** about **0.6 W/m2** in the mid-2000s and about **1.3 W/m2** in recent years (CERES EBAF 4.2.1, 2001-2024). The single-year peak in 2023 was about 1.8 W/m2 (secondary). Models give less than half the observed increase. The likely cause is lower reflectivity. Satellite coverage is at risk. https://eos.org/editor-highlights/earths-energy-imbalance-is-growing-faster-than-expected ; https://www.unsw.edu.au/newsroom/news/2025/07/earth-trapping-more-heat-than-forecast. VERIFIED (snippet)
- **WMO SoGC 2025:** EEI is a key indicator for the first time, and it reached a **new high in 2025**. The ocean takes up about 91% of the excess heat. https://wmo.int/node/38416. VERIFIED (snippet; no W/m2 value found). The IGCC 2025 also gives EEI at a record. VERIFIED (snippet)
- **IMO 2020 shipping sulfur:**
  - Jordan and Henry 2024 (Earth's Future): ERF **+0.139 +/- 0.019 W/m2**; **+0.046 C** over 2020-2029, about 2-3 years of extra warming. It helps explain 2023 but "other factors are needed". https://doaj.org/article/69479e59acac43adbb2681c718d4bd7a. VERIFIED (snippet)
  - Yuan et al. 2024 (Commun. Earth Environ., doi 10.1038/s43247-024-01442-3): **+0.2 +/- 0.11 W/m2** over the ocean only; about 0.16 K over about 7 years. https://link.springer.com/10.1038/s43247-024-01442-3. VERIFIED (snippet)
  - The GCM consensus is about **0.10 W/m2**. A 2026 preprint argues that sub-grid effects raise it to 0.19-0.31 (not peer-reviewed). https://egusphere.copernicus.org/preprints/2026/egusphere-2026-2654/. VERIFIED (snippet)

Result: **+0.07 +/- 0.05 C** global warming, "a main driver" of the faster rate since 2010. The effect is temporary. https://centaur.reading.ac.uk/123626 ; https://www.reading.ac.uk/news/2025/Research-News/Air-pollution-cuts-in-East-Asia-likely-accelerated-global-warming. VERIFIED (snippet)
- **Low-cloud albedo (Goessling, Rackow, Jung, Science, Dec 2024):** planetary albedo in 2023 was the lowest since at least 1940. Without the albedo drop since Dec 2020, 2023 would have been about **0.23 C cooler**. The main cause is less low cloud in the northern mid-latitudes and tropics, especially the NE Atlantic. Polar surface albedo explains only about 15%. The cause (variability, aerosol or cloud feedback) is unresolved. https://www.awi.de/en/about-us/service/press/single-view/sprunghafter-anstieg-der-erderwaermung-ist-massgeblich-auf-geringere-rueckstrahlkraft-des-planeten-zurueckzufuehren.html ; https://epic.awi.de/id/eprint/59831. VERIFIED (snippet)
